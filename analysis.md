# Analysis - CS50AI Attention Project

## Layer 5, Head 3 - Prepositional Object Relationships

This attention head focuses on the relationship between prepositions and the nouns they modify. Prepositions consistently attend strongly to the nouns that follow them.

Example Sentences:
- "The cat jumped **over** the [MASK] fence." → "over" shows strong attention to "fence"
- "She placed the book **on** the [MASK] table." → "on" strongly attends to "table"

## Layer 7, Head 8 - Adjective-Noun Agreement

This head shows adjectives attending to the nouns they modify. Adjectives show highest attention to their corresponding nouns.

Example Sentences:
- "The **beautiful** garden had a [MASK] fountain." → "beautiful" attends to "garden"
- "She wore a **red** dress to the [MASK] party." → "red" attends to "dress"
